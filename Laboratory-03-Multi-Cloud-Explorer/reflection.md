## Reflection

Exploring AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud gave me a better understanding of how cloud providers can offer similar technologies while having different areas of strength. The platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its connection to artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. These technologies are important for modern applications and made Google Cloud especially interesting to me.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have several similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, security, and monitoring services. They also allow organizations to scale their resources without having to purchase and maintain all of the physical hardware themselves. Although their services have different names and interfaces, many of them perform similar functions.

Business requirements play an important role when selecting a cloud provider. A company should consider its budget, existing infrastructure, employees' skills, application requirements, security concerns, location, and expected growth. For example, Azure can be a practical choice for an organization already using Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud can be attractive for an AI-focused company. AWS can also be useful for businesses that need a wide variety of cloud services.

Working as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that there is no universal cloud provider that is automatically the best for every client. A recommendation should come from analyzing the client's actual problem and matching it with the appropriate services.

My GitHub portfolio also became more organized after this mission. I added separate research documents, comparison information, client recommendations, screenshots, and a Linux investigation. This activity helped me practice both cloud technology research and technical documentation.
